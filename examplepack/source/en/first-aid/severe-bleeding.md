# Severe external bleeding

For life-threatening external bleeding, call emergency services as soon as possible. Apply firm, continuous direct pressure to the wound with a dressing or clean material.

Keep pressure on the wound until the bleeding stops, help arrives, or another person can take over. If the injury is on a limb and a tourniquet is available, use it only if you know how and according to your training.

Monitor the person's breathing and responsiveness while waiting for help.

Source: American Red Cross, "Bleeding (Life-Threatening External)".

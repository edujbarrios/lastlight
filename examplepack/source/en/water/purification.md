# Safe water during an emergency

If safe bottled water is not available, bring clear water to a rolling boil for 1 minute. At elevations above 6,500 feet (about 2,000 meters), boil water for 3 minutes. Let it cool and store it in clean, sanitized containers with tight covers.

If the water is cloudy, first filter it through a clean cloth, paper towel, or coffee filter, or allow sediment to settle and draw off the clear water before boiling.

Boiling or disinfecting will not make water safe if it contains fuel, toxic chemicals, or radioactive material. In that case, use bottled water or another safe source and follow instructions from local authorities.

Source: CDC, "How to Make Water Safe in an Emergency".
https://www.cdc.gov/water-emergency/about/how-to-make-water-safe.html
